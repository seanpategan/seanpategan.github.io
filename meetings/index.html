Meetings

If I've invited you to book a time with me, you can do it here. Pick an open slot and we'll both get a calendar invite.

Book a meeting →

How It Works

  1. Enter your email address. If it's on my list, you'll get a 6-digit sign-in code.
  2. Enter the code. That browser stays signed in for 7 days.
  3. Pick one of my open times. It checks my calendar, so you won't see times when I'm busy.
  4. We both get a calendar invitation by email, with a Jitsi Meet link. It opens in your web browser, with nothing to install. You can cancel from the same page.

Booking is invite only. If you'd like to meet and aren't on the list, email me.

Privacy

The booking page stores your email address, the time you book, and any note you add, plus a log of sign-in attempts for security. Nothing is shared. Email me if you want your data deleted.
